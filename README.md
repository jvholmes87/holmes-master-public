# Jason V. Holmes Research Portfolio

## Research statement

My research focuses on requirements engineering and obligation traceability in federal construction compliance. Small organizations can carry the same external obligation load as larger organizations while operating with less administrative tooling. I study how data models can enforce applicability, lifecycle, evidence, deadline, and renewal rules consistently across compliance work.

## Research program

The program examines one mechanism in two populations. The first is contractor-side obligation compliance for a small subcontractor performing federal construction task orders at an overseas installation. The second is supplier-side qualification burden for small manufacturers and suppliers seeking entry into aerospace and advanced manufacturing supply chains. Both populations must translate external requirements into controlled, traceable, and renewable evidence.
